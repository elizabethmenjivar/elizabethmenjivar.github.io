What predicts a college major’s salary?

The question

Across 172 majors in the FiveThirtyEight recent-grads data, which factors best explain differences in median salary: the share of women in a major, the unemployment rate, or the field of study?

What I did

  • Started with a simple regression, then a multiple regression with unemployment rate and major category.
  • Used stepwise selection (AIC) to pick predictors, and compared models with a nested ANOVA.
  • Checked the assumptions with residual plots, a Q-Q plot, Cook’s distance, and VIF.

What I found

  • Major category was the strongest predictor. Engineering stood out at about $20,000 above the baseline category.
  • The share of women stayed significant after adjusting for field. Its effect shrank from about $30,700 to $15,200 per full change in share.
  • Unemployment rate wasn’t significant once the other variables were in. The final model explains about 62% of the variation in median salary.

The goal

What it does
